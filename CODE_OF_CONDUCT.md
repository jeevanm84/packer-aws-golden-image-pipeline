# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free learning community. Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and focus discussion on the work.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, publication of private information, and deliberately unsafe technical guidance are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to [@jeevanm84](https://github.com/jeevanm84) through the contact method available on the maintainer's profile. Reports will be reviewed fairly and with respect for the reporter's privacy.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
